The problem

Procurement day shouldn't feel like a gamble

Most delays farmers face aren't about the crop — they're about not knowing where, when, or how long to wait.

Today

  • ❌ Long, unpredictable queues
  • ❌ Uncertain procurement dates
  • ❌ Paper-based, error-prone records
  • ❌ Centres overcrowded while others sit empty
  • ❌ No visibility into status once crop is dropped off
  • ❌ Payment timing is a mystery

With Kisan Setu

  • ✓ Smart, capacity-aware scheduling
  • ✓ Digital token with a fixed arrival window
  • ✓ Live queue position, updated in real time
  • ✓ Centre load balanced automatically
  • ✓ Stage-by-stage status, visible end to end
  • ✓ Transparent, trackable payment record
How it works

Eight steps, one continuous thread

  1. Register

    Name, mobile, village and district — verified by OTP.

  2. Add crop & land details

    Crop type, land area and expected harvest quantity.

  3. Request procurement

    Declare quantity and preferred date for a specific crop.

  4. Get a smart slot

    The system ranks nearby centres by distance, queue and capacity.

  5. Arrive with your token

    A digital token fixes your arrival window — no overnight queuing.

  6. Quality check & weighing

    Officers log moisture, grade and final weight against your token.

  7. Procurement confirmed

    Value is calculated and locked against the graded weight.

  8. Payment released

    Status updates the moment funds are credited to your account.
